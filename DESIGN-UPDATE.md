# VENIZOR implementation contract

Direction: light editorial studio. Preserve the logo, Geist typography and blue brand accent. Help buyers understand the offer and reach a real contact path.

- Landing sections occupy at least one viewport, growing naturally with content. No forced scroll snapping or clipped mobile content.
- White, paper and pale blue surfaces across every route, including tools, simulators, diagrams, engineering panels, product panels, industries and inner-page footers. The homepage retains the animated navy gradient footer. Use one Geist family and the shared tokens in `src/app/design-system.css`.
- Blog: an oversized editorial opening, one featured story, an illustrated article grid, search and filters. Use entrance and hover motion with reduced-motion alternatives; no invented activity.
- Contact: a clear project brief, real field progress, direct email, next steps and an animated envelope illustration with a pause control.
- Playbook: a guided six-step workspace, three starting presets, a live architecture blueprint and contextual review questions. Copy the selected stack as a plain-text brief, with a manual fallback when clipboard access fails.
- Project concepts: layered light product scenes, accessible selection controls and consistent blue/white artwork replace the dark 3D card ring.
- Retain keyboard access, visible focus, skip links, pause controls and native links.
- Remove unsupported client names, quotes, benchmark claims and compliance claims. Architecture examples stay explicitly illustrative.
- No invented founders, human authors, phone numbers or business registration details. These and live service connections are deferred at the user's request.
- Integrations remain disabled until configured. Success must mean a real server acknowledgement, never an email draft or a mock delivery.

Acceptance: production build; all public routes and eight service pages render; unique canonical, metadata and OG image; no mobile overflow at 320/390/768px; keyboard navigation and reduced motion; contact validation, failure and mocked delivery tests; inspect desktop and mobile screenshots.

Latest verification: 33 public routes, 106 page/state/viewport scans, 12 automated accessibility scans and 29 production browser checks passed. Run `npm run verify:design` after a production build. See `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` for visual references and `artifacts/refinement/verification.json` for the audit.
